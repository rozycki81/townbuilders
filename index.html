What we build

Three kinds of work. Point at one and the drawing redraws itself.

  • Custom homes

    1/4" = 1'-0"

    Ground-up houses on flat lots and hillsides. Grading, foundation, framing, rough-in, inspections, finish carpentry. One contract and one schedule from the first footing to the keys.

    Hillside lot? We read the soils report before we price the foundation.

  • Commercial build-outs

    1/8" = 1'-0"

    Tenant improvements, storefronts and offices. New partitions, ceilings, accessible restrooms, storefront glazing.

    We coordinate with the landlord's rep, your architect and the city inspector, so you deal with one contact.

  • Kitchens & baths

    1/2" = 1'-0"

    Cabinets set to a sixteenth. Showers waterproofed and flood-tested before the tile goes on.

How a job runs

01/ 04

  1. Site walk

    We come out, measure, photograph and ask a lot of questions. Then you get a written scope with line items you can compare against any other bid.

  2. Drawings & permit

    We build from your architect's set or bring in a designer and engineer. We submit to the city, answer plan-check comments and follow the permit until it's issued.

  3. Build

    Demo, foundation, framing, rough-in, insulation, drywall, finishes. The inspector signs off each stage before we cover it up.

  4. Final & punch list

    Final inspection, then a walkthrough with you and a roll of blue tape. Every item on the punch list gets closed out.
